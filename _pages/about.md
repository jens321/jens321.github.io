My research generally focuses on reinforcement learning and natural language processing, and more recently I’ve started thinking about self-improvement in AI agents.

**Bio**: I recently graduated from the <a href="http://nlp.cs.princeton.edu/">Princeton NLP</a> group, where I was advised by Professor [Karthik Narasimhan](https://karthikncode.github.io). My research focused on building decision-making agents, mainly using ideas from reinforcement learning (RL), imitation learning (IL), and natural language processing (NLP). To this end, I also closely collaborated with Professor [Benjamin Eysenbach](https://ben-eysenbach.github.io) as part of the [Princeton RL Lab](https://princeton-rl.github.io).
